Comfortaa-Bold.ttf, Quicksand-Bold.ttf, and DancingScript-Bold.otf are bundled
here (alongside DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf, see LICENSE-DEJAVU.txt) to give the pin
compositor genuine typeface variety without depending on whatever fonts
happen to be installed on the machine running the pipeline.
