DejaVu Sans Bold (DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf) is bundled here so text rendering is
identical across platforms (Windows/macOS/Linux) instead of depending on
whichever fonts happen to be installed on the machine running the pipeline.

DejaVu fonts are based on Bitstream Vera and are free for any use, including
redistribution and modification, with no royalty. Full license:
